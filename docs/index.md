## Conventions

- Claims are labelled **F** (sourced fact), **K** (product owner's input, to verify), **I** (inference), **U** (unknown).
- Other repos link here instead of copying: hub specs live in EV_PNC_hub-contracts, consent documents in EV_PnC_Consent_docs.
- Not published here on purpose: negotiation material, e-mail drafts, legal question sets and the K6 ceiling. They stay in the product owner's private files.
