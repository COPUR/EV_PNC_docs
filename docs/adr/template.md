# ADR-NNNN: Title in the imperative

**Status:** proposed | accepted | superseded by ADR-NNNN · **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

**Context.** What forces the decision: facts with their sources, constraints, what is unknown. Label claims F (sourced fact), I (inference), U (unknown).

**Decision.** What we will do, in one or two sentences, then the specifics.

**Options considered.** Each option with its main cost, and why it lost.

**Consequences.** What gets easier, what gets harder, what must change elsewhere (repos, gates, cards).

**Open.** Questions that stay open, with an owner.

**Sources.** Links or file paths.
