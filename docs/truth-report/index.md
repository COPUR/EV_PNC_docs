# Weekly truth report (card S-01)

One page every Friday: where each gate criterion (K1 to K9 and K1-H, see [go/no-go](../pilot/go-no-go.md)) really stands. The gate decision is 7 Dec 2026; the interim check is 9 Nov 2026.

## Rules

1. **Red first.** Criteria are listed failing or unknown before passing ones. Bad news is in the first line of the report.
2. **Every claim carries a label, an owner and a date.** F sourced fact (with the source), K product owner's input still to verify, I inference, U unknown. A criterion with no evidence is U and counts as not passed ("no evidence, no GO").
3. **No spin.** No "on track" without the date it is on track for; no "progress" without what changed since last week.
4. One page. Details go in linked cards, PRs or documents.
5. Written by the Architect agent from the board and the repos; the product owner reads it, and may correct facts but not wording of the status.
